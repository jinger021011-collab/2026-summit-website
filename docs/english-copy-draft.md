## 4. About the Summit

### Introductory Copy

The 2026 Time Series Tech Innovation Summit, themed “DB × AI,” will bring together experts from academia and industry, enterprise technology leaders, and industry practitioners for in-depth discussions on time series databases, time series foundation models, and industrial data intelligence.

The summit will unveil a new integrated product portfolio featuring TimechoDB and TimechoAI, and showcase applications of time series databases and artificial intelligence across aerospace, energy and power, oil and petrochemicals, and industrial manufacturing. Participants will jointly explore technology trends and industry opportunities arising from the deep convergence of databases and AI.
